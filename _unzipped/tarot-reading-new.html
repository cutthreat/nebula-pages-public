The word tarot originates from the Latin words Thora and Rota which mean East and West. In fact, it refers to combining symbolic reflections on past and future experiences.

Tarot readings are a way of exploring possible futures and personal themes symbolically. It combines intuition and the meaning of the cards. If you get a reading from the right tarot card reader, you may receive insights and reflections that feel meaningful to you.

One tarot deck has 78 cards. Among 78 cards, 22 are the Major Arcana, and 56 are the Minor Arcana cards. The Major Arcana cards represent significant symbolic stages or energies in life’s journey.

Each card contains a unique symbol with its own stories, imagery, and meanings. For instance, The Egyptian Tarot has an unusual meaning. It combines a standard tarot format and ancient Egyptian mythology.
